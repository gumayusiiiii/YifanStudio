SYSTEM_OVERVIEW

An AI-driven Dungeon Master that combines stable world lore, encounter logic, and a constrained narrative voice—aiming to feel authored and intentional rather than random chatbot output.

The engine organizes a campaign into three layers: world lore (locations, factions, rules), session memory (party choices, relationships, unresolved hooks), and a scene manager that decides what kind of beat the player should experience next.

Instead of letting the model improvise everything, each response is grounded in retrieved notes and encounter templates—keeping tone consistent while still allowing for surprise.

WORLD_BUILDING

Key locations and factions were first visualized as analog-style dungeon notes before being encoded into the AI system as structured lore.

DUNGEON_FLOW

ENTRY_STATE

Establishes tone, party stakes, and initial affordances without overwhelming the player.

BRANCHED_PATHS

Multiple viable routes encoded as soft rails—players can backtrack or jump tracks.

ESCALATION

Risk and stakes climb over time, but failure routes are written as content, not dead-ends.

NPC_MEMORY

Key characters track prior interactions so the AI can respond consistently in later scenes.

LOOPING_NODES

Certain hubs can be revisited with different outcomes as the party’s state evolves.

FINAL_BEAT

Closes the dungeon while leaving narrative threads the engine can pick up in future arcs.
