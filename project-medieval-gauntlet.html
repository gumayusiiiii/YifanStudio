MEDIEVAL GAUNTLET

// Handcrafted Wearable Armor / Metalwork / Mechanical Design

CRAFT_PHILOSOPHY

This project is an exploration of historical craftsmanship through modern making. By hand-forging each plate, I sought to understand the ergonomic challenges faced by medieval armorers.

The goal was not just visual replication, but functional authenticity—ensuring full range of motion while maintaining protective coverage.

IRON Primary structure for impact resistance
ALUMINUM Weight reduction in non-critical areas
LEATHER Inner glove foundation & flexibility
RIVETS Articulated joint connections
