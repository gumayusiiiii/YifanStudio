Mouse for hand function disabilities

ACCESS_NEEDS

This project re-imagines the form and interaction logic of the conventional computer mouse for people with hand impairments. Field research—interviews, observation, and video documentation— revealed three core pain points:

  1. Excessive travel distance — standard buttons sit beyond the users' comfortable reach.
  2. Dispersed force points — clicks require strength that impaired fingers cannot concentrate.
  3. One-size-fits-all geometry — commercial shells fail to conform to palms altered by injury or deformity.

MOUSE_TEMPLATE

VALUE_&_NEXT

This design meets the fundamental need for seamless digital interaction among users with physical disabilities and offers a reusable parametric workflow for inclusive consumer electronics.

Through systematic research, validation, and iteration, the project embodies the principle that "design is care"—turning empathy into tangible improvements in everyday life.
