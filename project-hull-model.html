HULL_STUDY

Carving an ancient sailing hull from discarded workshop offcuts, treating waste wood as a canvas for form, proportion, and light.

Working only with a carving knife, sandpaper, and a hand saw, this model turns scrap stock into a refined, tapering hull. The absence of power tools forces slower decisions—every cut leaves a trace of the maker’s hand.

The final object reads as both artifact and diagram: a study of waterline, keel, and frame relationships, but also a quiet sculpture about time, erosion, and the memory of built vessels.
