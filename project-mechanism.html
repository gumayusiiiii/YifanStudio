FIG. 01 / LINKAGE STUDY ALUMINUM + IRON

MECHANISM STUDY

Hand-fabricated linkage exploring how force, proportion, and riveted joints choreograph motion without electronics. Every member was cut, drilled, and tuned manually to amplify the tactile qualities of mechanical articulation.

CATEGORYMECHANICAL STUDY
YEAR2022
DURATION8 WEEKS
ROLEDESIGN / FABRICATION

1 // STRUCTURE
Aluminum rails define the neutral axis while iron straps articulate around it. Each strap is manually bent, introducing subtle deviations that give the motion a human cadence.

2 // MOTION
Riveted pivots create instant centers that hold their geometry even after repeated stress cycles. The absence of bearings makes the tactile feedback audible—every movement has a sound and weight.
